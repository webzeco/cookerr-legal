Privacy Policy – Cookerr

Effective Date: August 2025

Cookerr is a kitchen-sharing marketplace that allows users to find and rent kitchens by the hour. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email, password, and profile details.
  • Location Data: Approximate location to show nearby kitchens.
  • Booking & Payment Data: Transaction info processed securely via Stripe.
  • App Activity: Analytics on app usage, page views, and interactions.
  • Device Info: Identifiers collected by Firebase for crash reporting and analytics.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To process bookings and payments securely.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with service providers (e.g., Firebase, Google Maps, Stripe) to operate the app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. We implement security best practices to protect your information.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Some payment and legal records may be retained for compliance.

Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data. See our Delete Account page for details.
