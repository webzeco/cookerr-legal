Delete Your Cookerr Account

If you would like to permanently delete your Cookerr account and data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

  • Email us at privacy@cookerr.app from your registered account email.
  • Or, use the in-app option (Profile → Account → Delete Account).

What Data is Deleted

  • Your account profile and login information.
  • Your kitchen listings and booking history.
  • Your uploaded photos and content.
  • Your device identifiers and analytics data.

What May Be Retained

Payment and transaction records may be retained for up to 5 years to comply with legal and financial regulations.

Timeline

We process deletion requests within 7 days after verification.
